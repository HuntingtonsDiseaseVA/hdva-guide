Veterans Resources

resources for HD veterans — VA programs, home care, caregiver support, mental health, and financial assistance.

⚠️ Important: Each program listed here has specific eligibility criteria that must be met in order to qualify. Being a veteran with Huntington's Disease does not automatically qualify you for any of these programs. Some benefits — such as housing grants and adaptive equipment programs — may also require a documented service-connected disability rating, sometimes as high as 100%. Always verify your eligibility with your VA social worker before applying.

For the full Richmond VAMC contact list, see the VA Resource Guide for HD Veterans.
